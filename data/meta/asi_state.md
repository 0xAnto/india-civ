# asi_state

Reference year: 2023-24 (ASI Volume I, released 2025-09). Units: INR lakh for money, persons and factories for counts.
Source: `https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestreleasesfiles/1759232761372-ASI%20Vol%20I%202023-24C-2.pdf` (776 pages). Tables 2 and 4, column "All" of each state's first page.
Fields: factories, factories in operation, persons engaged, workers, fixed capital, total output, GVA, NVA.

Coverage: 36 of 36 states and UTs. Unmatched names: none (source spelling "Chattisgarh", "A & N Islands", "Dadra & N Haveli & Daman & Diu" mapped by alias).
Checks: all-India factories 260,061, GVA 245,833,606 lakh and persons engaged 19,589,131 (sum of states) agree with the PIB summary of ASI 2023-24 (the all-India GVA there is 245,833,605: rounding).
Caveat: factory sector only; GVA is booked where the factory sits.

Sources not reached: RBI Handbook Tables 116 to 133 (ASI by state): CAPTCHA page, see gsdp_state.md. Not needed.

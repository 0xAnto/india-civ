# fisheries_state: fish production by state, inland and marine, 2023-24

Files: data/normalized/fisheries_state.json. Script: pipeline/build_fisheries_livestock.py. Rows: 36 (all matched).

## Source
Department of Fisheries data, as printed in Agricultural Statistics at a Glance 2024-25, table 2.57 (unit: lakh tonnes; 2019-20 to 2023-24). The series field `total_series` holds the five years.
All-India 2023-24 in the table: 139.07 inland, 44.95 marine, 184.02 total.

## Caveats
- 2023-24 is provisional. Small states show 0.00 because of rounding to two decimals.
- The Handbook on Fisheries Statistics itself was not reachable (see the table below). The numbers come from the DA&FW book, which cites the Department of Fisheries.
